August 2026 Author Discussion – Disconnected, Eleanor Vincent

Tri-Valley Readers is thrilled to host a discussion with Eleanor Vincent, a Walnut Creek–based memoirist, in August 2026. We hope members and guests will read Disconnected ahead of time so we can make the most of this opportunity to speak with a local author in person.

Tri-Valley Readers hosted an in-person author meetup with Ayelet Waldman in July 2026 at local indie bookstore The Quirky Crow. Waldman joined our club to discuss her novel, A Perfect Hand.
