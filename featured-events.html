Featured Events

Special gatherings beyond our monthly book discussions — author visits, local events, and more.

July 2026 Author Discussion – A Perfect Hand, Ayelet Waldman

Tri-Valley Readers is excited to host an in-person author meetup with Ayelet Waldman in July 2026. Waldman will join our club to discuss her latest novel, A Perfect Hand—a witty historical fiction set in nineteenth-century England. We hope members and guests will read the book ahead of time and come ready for a lively conversation.

August 2026 Author Discussion – Disconnected, Eleanor Vincent

Tri-Valley Readers is thrilled to host a discussion with Eleanor Vincent, a Walnut Creek–based memoirist, in August 2026. Eleanor has generously agreed to meet with our club to talk about her latest book and the story behind it. We hope members and guests will read Disconnected ahead of time so we can make the most of this opportunity to speak with a local author in person.

This event is open to the public. Join us at the San Ramon Public Library on Wed., Aug. 26, 2026, from 5:30–7:30 pm.
